Reading this / data quality

  • October is a booking, not a closure. Those 28 Casa and 27 Casita nights were taken off-Airbnb and blocked to keep channels in sync, so they count as booked. The $10 Full Casa reservation across the same dates is the sync placeholder, not the revenue.
  • Short buckets never get flagged as over-pacing. Thanksgiving is 10 nights — one booking reads as 80% occupancy — so buckets under 14 nights show their score without the yellow flag.
  • The calendar is not open past 2027-05-29 — a 114-night block. Those nights are excluded from occupancy rather than counted available, which is why the far buckets are blank.
  • ΔMo and ΔLB are blank where the archive cannot answer. They need archive depth and will fill in as daily snapshots accumulate. Blank beats invented.
  • Compsets unchanged since the last snapshot, so the market series is continuous. Each listing's compset is named beside its table.
